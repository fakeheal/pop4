Most of the shows Amazon Prime puts out haven't really worked for me. I'm still a little salty about **The Wheel of Time** getting cancelled, and I mostly watch **Rings of Power** for the pretty scenery... On the other hand, **Fleabag** and **The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel** rank pretty high on my personal list.

So I went into Carrie with no expectations. Besides, what's left to say about Carrie? Stephen King's novel already has three screen adaptations ([the 1976 film](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0074285/), [the 2002 TV movie](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0319970/) and [the 2013 version with Chloë Grace Moretz](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1939659/)), plus a sequel about her half-sister, [The Rage: Carrie 2](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0144814/). Another version seemed pointless. Or at least that's what I thought.

It turns out that even a story told this many times still has something to add. The series moves Carrie into a present-day American high school, and that's the scariest thing about it. Not the supernatural powers, but the everyday life of a teenager.

Bullying doesn't end with the last bell anymore. It starts in the hallway and carries on in your phone: every humiliation can be filmed, shared and commented on before Carrie even gets home. There's nowhere to hide, because school is everywhere now.

And behind a lot of the cruelty there's something even sadder: it's all done for the views. Followers, likes, who's "in" and who's left out are all currency. A video of someone else's humiliation is just content.

Add to that the drills on how to hide if someone walks into school with a gun. For the students, they're just part of the school week. And the drugs and alcohol, which nobody really bothers to hide. You end up with a picture where the supernatural is barely needed to make it scary.

One of the least realistic scenes for me was the gym packed with "concerned" parents. In this world, the one parent who actually cares is... Carrie's mother.

And she was the surprise for me. At one point I was even ready to take Margaret's side (Samantha Sloyan). After everything you see in that school, a woman who wants to hide her daughter from the sinful world out there doesn't seem so crazy anymore. Her methods are awful, but the horrors are real...

The acting is great! Nobody felt fake to me, or failed to deliver what their character is in the show for. I hadn't seen [Summer H. Howell](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm5640132/) before, but she was phenomenal as Carrie: innocent, well-meaning and open to the world, even though she's been hidden from it all her life. The scene where she talks to Peter Morton ([Tim Bagley](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0046730/)) after **the shower scene** really shows off her incredible range.

{{< spoiler "Spoilers: the prom" >}}
The prom scene, the most famous moment of the whole story, felt pretty underwhelming to me. I'm guessing that's because the creators wanted Carrie to stay the hero until the very end, instead of turning into a monster. Or maybe the villain was high school all along. 🙂

The funniest part: my girlfriend hadn't seen anything and knew nothing about Carrie, and I'd been hyping her up for the finale the whole time...
{{< /spoiler >}}

Still, there's going to be [a second season](https://www.tvline.com/2294196/carrie-renewed-season-2-prime-video/), and I'll most likely watch it! And while I wait, I might finally read the book, since I never have. :)

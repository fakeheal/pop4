If you finished Loki season 2 and wanted more, this is basically it. **TVA** is a five-issue miniseries set right after the show. Mobius is back at work at a brand-new TVA, and B-15 and a reprogrammed Miss Minutes are recruiting strays from broken timelines: Ghost-Spider, Captain Carter, a heartbroken Gambit and more. Then a mysterious killer in a green cloak starts hunting agents inside the TVA itself. It's a whodunit at the end of time, which is a very Loki way to spend five issues.

It's written by Katharyn Blair, who was a writer on Loki season 2, and you can tell. The TVA feels exactly like the show: the retro offices, the bureaucracy, the jokes. This was her Marvel Comics debut (she's also a novelist), and the art is by Pere Pérez, who has drawn a lot of Spider-books, so Gwen is in good hands.

My favourite parts were the small ones. The conversation between Scarlet Witch and Ghost-Spider was the emotional heart of the series for me. Two people who've lost their worlds, talking it out. And I loved the little "interviews" Miss Minutes does at the start of each issue. They're funny, a bit creepy, and very her.

The downside: it ends abruptly. Just as it feels like the story is getting going, it's over. I'd still recommend it if you're a Loki fan, because it really does read like a continuation of the series. Just don't get too attached. I doubt anyone at the MCU will ever honour whatever happened here. 🙂
